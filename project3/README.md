# Project 3: Delegation Record — Laptop Research

A worked example of the Agent Factory **Delegation Record**: writing a brief, delegating a real research job to an AI assistant, checking its claims, and recording the decision.

**Job:** Pick a laptop for university under PKR 150,000
**Date:** 21 Oct 2026
**Assistant:** Claude
**Decision:** Accept

## Files

| File | What it is |

| [`delegation-record.md`](delegation-record.md) | The completed Delegation Record (brief, run, check, decision, what to save) |
| [`template/delegation-record-template.md`](template/delegation-record-template.md) | The blank Delegation Record template |
| [`brief/brief-template.md`](brief/brief-template.md) | The reusable six-part brief with placeholders |
| [`brief/laptop-brief.md`](brief/laptop-brief.md) | The filled-in brief that was delegated |

## Key lesson

The brief's constraints could not all be met at once: no **new** 16 GB laptop with a **local warranty** was available in Pakistan at **PKR 150,000 or under** in October 2026. The useful behaviour was surfacing that conflict and showing one option per broken constraint, instead of silently dropping one.

Next time: check that the constraints can be met together, and say in the brief which one may bend first.
