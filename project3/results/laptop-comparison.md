## Notes

- **Option A:** Priceoye lists Rs 159,999 (was Rs 169,999) but marks the listing "DISCONTINUED". Shophive showed it in stock at Rs 159,999 with a 7-day replacement warranty, seen only in a search result. Availability: **unverified**.
- **Option B:** Intag lists Rs 115,000 (was Rs 130,000), in stock, and states a 1-year warranty on every laptop. Battery health unknown, which matters for the full-day requirement.
- **Why nothing new fits:** Mega.pk put the cheapest 16 GB laptop in Pakistan at PKR 214,999 for October 2026. Galaxy.pk listed the IdeaPad Slim 3 (i5, 16 GB) at Rs 200,000 on international warranty.
- **Unconfirmed lead:** a search snippet showed a Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3 i7-13620H, 16 GB, at Rs 162,500 on Paklap, but Paklap's current listing shows the local-warranty version at Rs 235,000. **Unverified.**

## Recommendation

If you can stretch about PKR 10,000 over budget, get the Infinix Y3H Plus: it's the only new 16 GB / 512 GB machine near the budget, but confirm warranty and stock with the seller before paying. If PKR 150,000 is a hard limit, the used EliteBook 850 G7 meets the spec for PKR 115,000; ask Intag for a battery-health report first.
