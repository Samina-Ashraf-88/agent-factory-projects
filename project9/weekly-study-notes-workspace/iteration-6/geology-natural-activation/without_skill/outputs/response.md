# Geology Week 1: Study Notes

## 1. What Is Geology?
**Geology** is the scientific study of Earth. It covers four areas:
- **Materials**: what Earth is made of (minerals, rocks)
- **Structure**: how Earth is arranged (its layers)
- **History**: how Earth has changed over time
- **Processes**: what drives change (e.g., plate tectonics, the rock cycle)

---

## 2. Minerals vs. Rocks

| | **Mineral** | **Rock** |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Naturally occurring, inorganic solid | An aggregate (mixture) of one or more minerals |
| Composition | Definite chemical composition | Can vary |
| Structure | Ordered internal (crystalline) structure | A collection of mineral grains |

**A mineral must be all five of these:**
1. Naturally occurring
2. Inorganic
3. Solid
4. Definite chemical composition
5. Ordered internal structure

💡 *Think of minerals as the ingredients and rocks as the recipe.*

---

## 3. The Three Rock Types

| Rock Type | How It Forms | Key Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Igneous** | Magma (underground) or lava (at the surface) cools and solidifies | "Fire-formed" |
| **Sedimentary** | Sediment is **deposited → compacted → cemented**, or forms through other sediment-forming processes | Built up in layers |
| **Metamorphic** | Existing rock is changed by **heat, pressure, or chemically active fluids** | "Changed form" |

💡 **Memory aids:**
- *Igneous* comes from Latin *ignis*, "fire."
- *Metamorphic* means "change of form" (think *metamorphosis*).
- Sedimentary steps: **D-C-C**, "Deposit, Compact, Cement."

---

## 4. The Rock Cycle
- Describes how rocks **transform from one type to another** over **geological time**.
- Any rock type can become any other type, given the right conditions:
  - Melting and cooling → **igneous**
  - Weathering, deposition, compaction, cementation → **sedimentary**
  - Heat, pressure, fluids → **metamorphic**

---

## 5. Plate Tectonics
- Explains the **movement of Earth's lithospheric plates**.
- The lithosphere is Earth's rigid outer shell, broken into plates.

---

## 6. Earth's Layers (outside → inside)
1. **Crust**: thin outer layer
2. **Mantle**: thick middle layer
3. **Core**: center of Earth

💡 *"Crust, Mantle, Core" goes from the outside in, like the skin, flesh, and pit of a peach.*
