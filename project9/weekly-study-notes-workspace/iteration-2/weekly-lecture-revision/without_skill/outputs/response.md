## 1. ⭐ Causes of the Revolution (MIDTERM)

| Cause | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Financial crisis** | Huge state debt from wars, including the **American War of Independence** |
| **Bad harvests** | Poor harvests → **high bread prices** → hunger and unrest |
| **Enlightenment ideas** | Thinkers like **Rousseau** and **Montesquieu** challenged absolute monarchy and traditional authority |

💡 *Tip:* Be ready to explain how these causes **connected**. For example, debt made the crown desperate for money, so it called the Estates-General. Hunger made ordinary people more willing to act, and Enlightenment ideas gave them a way to justify it.

## 2. Timeline of Key Events

| Date | Event | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **May 1789** | **Estates-General** meets | First meeting since **1614** |
| **June 1789** | Third Estate breaks away → **National Assembly** | Commoners claim to represent the nation |
| **20 June 1789** | **Tennis Court Oath** | National Assembly vows not to disband until France has a constitution |
| **14 July 1789** | **Storming of the Bastille** | Symbol of revolt against royal power; now **France's national day** |
| **Aug 1789** | **Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen** | Liberty, equality, popular sovereignty |
| **1793–94** | **Reign of Terror** | Led by Robespierre and the Committee of Public Safety; ~**17,000** official executions |
| **July 1794** | **Robespierre executed** (Thermidor) | End of the Terror |
| **1799** | **Napoleon's coup** | Usually taken as the **end of the Revolution** |

---

## 3. Key Concepts

**The Three Estates**
1. **First Estate:** clergy
2. **Second Estate:** nobility
3. **Third Estate:** everyone else, about **97% of the population**

**Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen.** Its core principles:
- Liberty
- Equality
- Popular sovereignty (power comes from the people)

---

## 4. Key People and Groups

- **Rousseau and Montesquieu:** Enlightenment thinkers whose ideas influenced the Revolution
- **Robespierre:** leading figure of the Reign of Terror, executed in July 1794
- **Committee of Public Safety:** the body that ran the Terror
- **Napoleon:** his 1799 coup ended the revolutionary period
