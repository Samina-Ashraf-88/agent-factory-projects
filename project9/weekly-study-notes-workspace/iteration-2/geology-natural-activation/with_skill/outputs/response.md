# Geology — Week 1 Study Notes

## 1. What Geology Is
- Geology is the scientific study of Earth: its materials, structure, history, and processes.

**Key terms**
- **Geology**: the scientific study of Earth, its materials, structure, history, and processes.

## 2. Minerals and Rocks
- Minerals are solids that are naturally occurring and inorganic. They have a definite chemical composition and an ordered internal structure.
- Rocks are aggregates of one or more minerals.

**Key terms**
- **Mineral**: a naturally occurring inorganic solid with a definite chemical composition and an ordered internal structure.
- **Rock**: an aggregate of one or more minerals.

## 3. The Three Main Rock Types
- The three main rock types are igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic.

### Igneous Rocks
- Form when magma or lava cools and solidifies.

### Sedimentary Rocks
- Form through deposition, compaction, and cementation, or through other sediment-forming processes.

### Metamorphic Rocks
- Form when existing rocks change because of heat, pressure, or chemically active fluids.

**Key terms**
- **Igneous rock**: rock that forms when magma or lava cools and solidifies.
- **Sedimentary rock**: rock that forms through deposition, compaction, and cementation, or other sediment-forming processes.
- **Metamorphic rock**: rock that forms when existing rocks change because of heat, pressure, or chemically active fluids.

## 4. The Rock Cycle
- The rock cycle describes how rocks can change from one type to another over geological time.

**Key terms**
- **Rock cycle**: how rocks can transform from one type to another over geological time.

## 5. Plate Tectonics
- Plate tectonics explains the movement of Earth's lithospheric plates.

**Key terms**
- **Plate tectonics**: the explanation of how Earth's lithospheric plates move.
- **Lithospheric plates**: the plates of Earth whose movement plate tectonics explains.

## 6. Earth's Layers
- Earth's major layers are the crust, mantle, and core.

**Key terms**
- **Crust**: one of Earth's three major layers.
- **Mantle**: one of Earth's three major layers.
- **Core**: one of Earth's three major layers.
