# Geology: Week 1 Study Notes

## 1. What is Geology?
**Geology** is the scientific study of Earth. It covers:
- **Materials**: what Earth is made of
- **Structure**: how those materials are arranged
- **History**: how Earth has changed over time
- **Processes**: the forces that shape Earth

## 2. Minerals vs. Rocks

| | **Minerals** | **Rocks** |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition** | Naturally occurring, inorganic solids | Aggregates (combinations) of one or more minerals |
| **Composition** | Definite chemical composition | Variable; depends on which minerals are present |
| **Structure** | Ordered internal (crystalline) structure | A mix of mineral grains |

🔑 **Memory tip:** Minerals are the *ingredients* and rocks are the *recipes*.

**The 4 requirements for a mineral:**
1. Naturally occurring
2. Inorganic
3. Solid
4. Definite chemical composition with an ordered internal structure

## 3. The Three Main Rock Types

| Rock Type | How It Forms | Key Words |
|---|---|---|
| **Igneous** | Magma (below the surface) or lava (at the surface) cools and solidifies | Cooling, solidifying, "fire" rock |
| **Sedimentary** | Sediments are deposited, compacted, and cemented together (or formed by other sediment-forming processes) | Deposition → compaction → cementation |
| **Metamorphic** | Existing rocks are changed by heat, pressure, or chemically active fluids | Transformation, "changed form" |

🔑 **Memory tip:** Sedimentary rocks form in this order: **D-C-C** (Deposition, Compaction, Cementation).

⚠️ **Common confusion:** Metamorphic rocks change *without fully melting*. If a rock melts completely and then cools, the result is **igneous**.

## 4. The Rock Cycle
- Describes how rocks **transform from one type to another** over geological time.
- Any rock type can become any other type, given the right conditions.

```
        Igneous
       ↗       ↘
 (melting)   (weathering, deposition)
     ↑             ↓
Metamorphic ← Sedimentary
        (heat & pressure)
```
*(Simplified: other paths also exist. For example, igneous rocks can become metamorphic directly.)*

## 5. Plate Tectonics
- The theory that explains the **movement of Earth's lithospheric plates**.
- The **lithosphere** is Earth's rigid outer shell, broken into plates.

## 6. Earth's Major Layers
From the outside in:
1. **Crust**: the thin outermost layer
2. **Mantle**: the thick middle layer
3. **Core**: the center

🔑 **Memory tip:** "**C**an **M**ike **C**ook?" = Crust, Mantle, Core
