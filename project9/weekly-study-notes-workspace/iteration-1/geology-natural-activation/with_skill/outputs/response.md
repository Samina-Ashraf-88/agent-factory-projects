# Geology — Week 1 Study Notes

## 1. Introduction to Geology
- Geology is the scientific study of Earth, including its materials, structure, history, and processes.

**Key terms**
- **Geology**: the scientific study of Earth, its materials, structure, history, and processes.

## 2. Minerals and Rocks
- Minerals are naturally occurring inorganic solids. Each has a definite chemical composition and an ordered internal structure.
- Rocks are aggregates of one or more minerals.

**Key terms**
- **Mineral**: a naturally occurring inorganic solid with a definite chemical composition and an ordered internal structure.
- **Rock**: an aggregate of one or more minerals.

## 3. The Three Main Rock Types
- There are three main rock types: igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic.
- **Igneous rocks** form when magma or lava cools and solidifies.
- **Sedimentary rocks** form through deposition, compaction, and cementation, or through other sediment-forming processes.
- **Metamorphic rocks** form when existing rocks change because of heat, pressure, or chemically active fluids.

**Key terms**
- **Igneous rock**: rock formed when magma or lava cools and solidifies.
- **Sedimentary rock**: rock formed through deposition, compaction, and cementation, or other sediment-forming processes.
- **Metamorphic rock**: rock formed when existing rocks are changed by heat, pressure, or chemically active fluids.

## 4. The Rock Cycle
- The rock cycle describes how rocks can transform from one type to another over geological time.

**Key terms**
- **Rock cycle**: the way rocks can transform from one type to another over geological time.

## 5. Earth's Structure and Plate Tectonics
- Earth's major layers are the crust, mantle, and core.
- Plate tectonics explains the movement of Earth's lithospheric plates.

**Key terms**
- **Plate tectonics**: the theory that explains the movement of Earth's lithospheric plates.
- **Earth's major layers**: the crust, mantle, and core.
