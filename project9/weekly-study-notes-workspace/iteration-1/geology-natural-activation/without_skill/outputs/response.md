# Geology: Week 1 Study Notes

## 1. What Is Geology?
**Geology** is the scientific study of Earth, covering its:
- **Materials** (what it's made of)
- **Structure** (how it's arranged)
- **History** (how it changed over time)
- **Processes** (what shapes it)

---

## 2. Minerals vs. Rocks

| | **Mineral** | **Rock** |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Naturally occurring, inorganic solid | Aggregate (mixture) of one or more minerals |
| Composition | Definite chemical composition | Variable |
| Structure | Ordered internal (crystalline) structure | Made of mineral grains |

**The 5 criteria for a mineral:**
1. Naturally occurring
2. Inorganic
3. Solid
4. Definite chemical composition
5. Ordered internal structure

> **Memory tip:** Minerals are the "ingredients"; rocks are the "recipe."

---

## 3. The Three Rock Types

| Rock Type | How It Forms | Key Words |
|---|---|---|
| **Igneous** | Magma or lava **cools and solidifies** | Fire, melting, cooling |
| **Sedimentary** | Sediments are **deposited, compacted, and cemented** (or form through other sediment-forming processes) | Layers, deposition |
| **Metamorphic** | Existing rocks are **changed by heat, pressure, or chemically active fluids** | Transformation, "meta" = change |

**Note:** Magma is molten rock *below* the surface. Lava is molten rock that has reached the surface.

---

## 4. The Rock Cycle
- Describes how rocks **change from one type to another** over geological time.
- Any rock type can become any other type, depending on the processes acting on it:
  - Melting and cooling → **igneous**
  - Weathering, deposition, compaction, cementation → **sedimentary**
  - Heat, pressure, fluids → **metamorphic**

---

## 5. Plate Tectonics
- The theory that explains how Earth's **lithospheric plates** move.
- The lithosphere is Earth's rigid outer shell, broken into plates.

---

## 6. Earth's Layers
From the outside in:
1. **Crust**: the thin outermost layer
2. **Mantle**: the thick middle layer
3. **Core**: the center of Earth
