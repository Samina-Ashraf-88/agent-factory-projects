ECON 101 – week 4 (mon + wed + fri lectures)

demand: law of demand = as price rises qty demanded falls, ceteris paribus
demand curve slopes downward
determinants of demand (shift the curve): income, prices of related goods (substitutes, complements), tastes, expectations, number of buyers
normal good - demand up when income up. inferior good - demand down when income up (e.g. instant noodles)
supply: law of supply - price up -> qty supplied up
supply determinants: input prices, technology, expectations, number of sellers
movement along curve = change in price. shift of curve = change in determinant. VERY IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
equilibrium = where Qd = Qs. eq price aka market clearing price
surplus when price above eq, shortage when price below
price elasticity of demand: PED = %ΔQd / %ΔP
|PED| > 1 elastic, < 1 inelastic, = 1 unit elastic
inelastic examples: insulin, gasoline short run
midpoint method: %Δ = (new-old)/((new+old)/2) x 100
price ceiling = legal max price (rent control) -> shortage if below eq
price floor = legal min (minimum wage) -> surplus if above eq
Q from class: why does a binding price ceiling cause a shortage? (answer: qd > qs at the capped price)
consumer surplus = willingness to pay - price paid. producer surplus = price received - cost
total surplus = CS + PS, maximized at equilibrium
remember: demand curve slopes downward!!
