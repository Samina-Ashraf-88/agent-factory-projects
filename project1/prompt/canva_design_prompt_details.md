**Canvas Dimensions**: 1080x1350 pixels (Standard LinkedIn Portrait format, optimized for mobile readability)
**Typography**: Montserrat (Bold, for headings/titles), Inter (Medium/Regular, for body copy)
**Color Palette**: 
- Primary: LinkedIn Blue (`#0077B5`)
- Accent 1: Tech Teal (`#00BFA5`)
- Accent 2: Cool Gray (`#5A6268`)
