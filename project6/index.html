How to play

  1. Steer your snake and eat fruit to grow. Each fruit is +10 points and +1 length.
  2. 💥A snake dies if its head hits a wall, itself, or another snake's body. Make rivals crash into you for a +50 takedown.
  3. ⚔️Head-on collision: the longer snake survives. Equal length? Both die. All snakes move at the same instant, so every collision is resolved fairly and simultaneously.
  4. ⏱Sudden death: after about a minute — or once only two snakes remain — the walls close in one ring at a time. The flashing edge shows where the wall lands next: a head caught there is crushed, a body caught there is sliced off.
  5. 🏆Be the last snake alive to win (+100 bonus). The leaderboard ranks everyone by length.
☠ Special rule — the dead become food.
When any snake dies — rival or you — its whole body bursts into remains. Every piece is +5 points and +1 length for whoever gets there first. Big snakes leave big feasts.
