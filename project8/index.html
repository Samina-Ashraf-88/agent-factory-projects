Featured project

AI Meeting Assistant

A meeting goes in. Decisions, an action-item list, Trello cards and a daily briefing come out, with no copying and pasting.

  1. Read the meeting. Pulls the transcript from Fathom, a file, or pasted text.
  2. Summarise it. Writes a brief with decisions, action items with owners, and open questions.
  3. Create the tasks. One Trello card per action item, with owner, due date and priority label.
  4. Report daily. A Morning Briefing at 08:00 and an End-of-Day report at 18:00 on weekdays.

My role

I planned the work as four milestones and tracked them on their own Trello board. I connected Fathom and Trello through a single managed-OAuth key and chose where the reports are delivered. I directed an AI coding agent to build five custom skills, then checked their output against a sample meeting.
