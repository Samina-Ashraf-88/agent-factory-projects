# Brief template: 7-day study plan

```
Outcome:      a study plan for [your subject] for the next seven days.
Context:      [hours available, when in the day, what you have finished].
Constraints:  no more than [hours] a day. One rest day.
Authority:    choose the order of topics. Ask me before including
              anything paid.
Deliverable:  a seven-row table: day, topic, time, the resource to use.
Verification: every resource must have a link I can open.
```
