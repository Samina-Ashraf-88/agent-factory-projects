# Project 4: Delegation Record — Weekly AI Agents News

A second worked example of the Agent Factory **Delegation Record**: a research brief for a weekly news summary, delegated to an AI assistant, checked, and recorded.

**Job:** Weekly summary of AI agents news (14–21 Oct 2026)
**Date:** 21 Oct 2026
**Assistant:** Claude
**Decision:** Accept

## Key lesson

The Authority line ("say when two sources disagree") did its job: TechCrunch and Reuters gave different counts for the same Anthropic disclosures (three vs at least four), and the summary flagged it instead of picking one.

Next time: turn "last seven days" into exact dates, name the sub-topics, and ask for primary sources over aggregators.
